§ 01

What ChatGPT forgets,
talkamore remembers.

A thinking partner that can’t remember isn’t really thinking with you — it’s just answering. Most chats begin from nothing: a courteous stranger, every single time.

Without memory

The blank-slate bot

You re-explain your whole life each session. What you get back is continuity, a sense of being known, a thread to pull on next week — all gone the moment the tab closes.

With Maya

A partner who keeps the thread

She links today to something from two months ago, notices what keeps returning, and tells you the truth — gently, but honestly.

§ 04

Not a chatbot. A presence
that grows with you.

Maya isn’t built to keep you scrolling, nor to tell you what you want to hear. She’s built to understand you over time — and to be honest enough to be useful.

i.

Honest,
not flattering

She’ll gently disagree, push back, and name the thing you’ve been avoiding. A yes-machine never helped anyone think.

ii.

Continuous,
not episodic

No “remind me what we discussed.” She picks up exactly where you left off — even three weeks and four moods later.

iii.

Yours,
and private

Your memory belongs to you. A place to think out loud — not a feed, not a profile, not training fodder.
